# Major Project Reflection

## What advice would you give to yourself if you were to start a project like this again or make a similar project in the future?

- Firstly, I would tell him to make a rough outline and "STICK" to it. What happened with us was that when something basic had a problem we would get very deep into solving it and not be able to complete other things which brings me to the next advice.
- Distribute time wisely and set your priorities. Do not prioritize spending time working on something that is not essential for your project to stand out, over something that is. 
- Also for future projects, always keep a deadline in mind. Working without a time constraint will lead to inconsistency and no clear vision in mind.
